Privacy Policy

La Lavandería · ZamLab Studios · Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Versión en español

In short: the game does not ask for an account, does not collect personal data, does not connect to the internet, and has no ads, analytics or purchases. It only stores your progress, your in-game coins and your settings on your own device.

1. Who we are

ZamLab Studios ("we") develops the game La Lavandería ("the game"), in which a young laundry girl serves the neighborhood monsters at night. For any question about this policy, write to zamlab.studios@gmail.com.

2. What is stored and where

The game stores, in your device's local storage:

  • the nights you have unlocked, your stars and your best result for each night;
  • the coins in your piggy bank (the in-game currency you earn by serving customers) and the upgrades you bought with them in the in-game shop;
  • your "Endless Shift" records (orders, wave and coins);
  • whether you have seen the tutorial, the character you chose (laundry girl or laundry boy), and whether sound and vibration are on or off;
  • the shift in progress, so you can resume it if the phone closes the game, and a list of the latest shifts already paid so they are never paid twice.

Coins can only be earned by playing: they cannot be bought with real money. The game never asks for your name. This information is not sent to us or to any third party. It stays on your device.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers or usage data. The game has no user accounts, shows no ads and includes no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Permissions

The Android app only requests the vibration permission, so the phone can vibrate at some moments of the game (it is switched off together with the sound, on the title screen or in the pause menu). It does not request internet access: the whole game ships inside the app and plays offline. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

The "More games" and "Privacy" buttons only open the AppGallery store or your browser, outside the game, when you tap them.

5. Purchases

The game is free and complete: the five nights, the "Endless Shift" and the upgrade shop, which is paid only with coins earned by playing. There are no real-money purchases. If any are added in the future, we will update this policy before they become available.

6. Third parties

The game does not share information with third parties. If you download it from an app store (for example, AppGallery), the store may process data about your download under its own policies, which are not ours. If you play a web version, the site hosting it may log technical visits (such as the IP address), like any website; the game adds no analytics.

7. Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The game features cartoon-style monsters (ghosts, a werewolf, a mummy, a vampire and jumping socks), with no blood or violence; check the age rating shown in the store before installing it.

8. Your control: deleting your data

You can delete the game's saved data at any time from Android settings (Settings → Apps → La Lavandería → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Since we have no copy of your information, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete.

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by the device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change affects how your information is handled (for example, if the game ever included purchases or ads), we will say so on this page before it takes effect.

11. Contact
